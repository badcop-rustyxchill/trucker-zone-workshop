Drag with the left mouse button on empty space to look around, or hold the right mouse button for mouse look. Use WASD to fly, Q/E or Space to move down/up, and Shift to move faster. Hold Ctrl for 0.01 m gizmo snapping and 1° rotation snapping. Click empty space to deselect. While looking, the wheel adjusts fly speed. Escape releases the mouse. Orbit controls are available using the Orbit button.
